The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, made at home with a small bar.

Program 01 · Taste, then adjust

Balance & technique

We make the same drink at two ratios, side by side, until the difference is obvious. Then technique: how long to shake, when to stir, and how much water ends up in the glass — which is the variable most home bartenders never think about and professionals obsess over.

  • 10 weeks
  • Beginners
  • Two drinks a week
  • A shaker and a jigger

Program 02 · Families, not recipes

The classics

Most cocktails are variations on a handful of templates. Learn the families and a recipe list stops being something to memorize: you can taste a new drink and know what it is, and improvise sensibly with what you have.

  • 12 weeks
  • Basics done
  • Two drinks a week
  • Three spirits

Start this program

Program 03 · Worth making yourself

Syrups & garnish

Homemade syrups cost pennies and transform drinks. We cover simple and rich syrups, flavored versions, keeping citrus fresh, making clear or large-format ice at home, and garnish chosen for aroma rather than decoration.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 hours a week
  • A saucepan

Start this program

Program 04 · Be at your own party

Hosting & batching

Making twelve cocktails to order means missing your own party. We build batched drinks that hold, plan a short menu that covers most preferences, set up a small home bar, and cover hosting responsibly — food, water, non-alcoholic options and knowing when to stop pouring.

  • 6 weeks
  • Comfortable making drinks
  • 2 hours a week
  • Bottles and a fridge
