How we work

Adults only, and sensible about it

This is for people 21 and over. Zaveloi sells and ships no alcohol, and takes no commission from any brand, so nothing here is steering you toward a bottle someone paid to promote.

Cocktails should be small, slow and occasional. We build menus with good non-alcoholic options, we talk about serving guests responsibly, and if drinking is something you're better off without, this isn't the hobby for you.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

How many bottles do I need?

Three to start: a whiskey or rum, a gin or tequila, and a vermouth. Plus citrus and sugar. That covers most of the classics.

Do you sell or ship alcohol?

No, never. You buy locally, and we take no commission from any brand or shop.

Is this bartending certification?

No. We coach home cocktail making. Bar licensing and alcohol service certification vary by state and are a separate matter entirely.

Can I do this without drinking much?

Yes. Drinks are small, you can split or share them, and we cover non-alcoholic versions of most templates.

My drinks always taste too sweet.

Very common, and usually syrup volume rather than the spirit. Once you taste two ratios side by side it's easy to correct.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Want drinks that taste balanced?

Twenty minutes on your shelf and where to start. Free, and 21+.
